---
title: "Getting Started"
description: "Set up your products and inventory in minutes"
---

<Info>
  Prerequisites:

  1. Connect your Shopify or WooCommerce store if you sell online
  2. Invite your team if you don't handle operations alone
</Info>

After completing this guide, you'll have everything set up and ready to take real orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your products" titleSize="h3">
    Add your products with details and images to get your logistics ready, whether you're adding them one by one, in bulk, or importing directly from your online store.

    Let's start with adding a single product manually:

    1. Go to the SKU Listings page and click on "Add New SKU"
    2. Choose "Add SKUs Manually"
    3. Fill in the product details
       1. Your product's name
       2. Product images

          <Tip>
            We recommend you add at least 2 high quality images.
          </Tip>
       3. A unique SKU ID for each item

          <Note>
            An [**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**](/glossary/SKU) is a unique code you assign to each product variant in your inventory. It helps you track items, manage stock, and avoid mistakes during fulfillment.  Example: a green medium t-shirt would have the SKU ID`TSHIRT-GRN-M`, and the green large t-shirt would have the SKU ID `TSHIRT-GRN-L`.
          </Note>
    4. View your products in the SKU Listings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your inventory" titleSize="h3">
    Stocking up your items in our warehouse lets us deliver your order as soon as it is created.

    1. Go to the Send Inventory page and click on "Send Inventory"
    2. Choose "Add Manually"
    3. Add the quantity you're sending in for each item in the list, then click Save & Continue
    4. Choose "Request Vehicle" if you'd like us to pick up your items for you, or "Send Inventory" if you'd prefer to send your products yourself to our warehouse
    5. Review your order, then click on "Send Request" to confirm your shipment
    6. Track your inbound order in the Inventory page

    We'll let you know once your items have arrived at our warehouse and ready to take orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first order" titleSize="h3">
    The last step to start business is to create an order and get it delivered to your customer.

    1. Go to the Orders page and click on "Add Order"
    2. Choose "Add Single Order" from the dropdown
    3. Enter in your customer's information including their:
       1. Full name
       2. Contact number
       3. Shipping address
    4. Add your products information to the order and include:
       1. Quantity
       2. Price
    5. Confirm the receipt
    6. Click on "Place Order" to confirm the order
  </Step>
</Steps>
