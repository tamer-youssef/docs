---
title: "Create an order"
description: "Place your orders through our portal whether manually, in bulk, or syncing your online store."
---

Create orders from our portal, and we'll handle it from there. Follow the steps below to create an order from within the portal, or sync with your online store.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Create single orders">
    1. Go to the Orders page and click on "Add Order"
    2. Choose "Add Single Order" from the dropdown
    3. Enter in your customer's information including their:
       1. Full name
       2. Contact number
       3. Shipping address
    4. Add your products information to the order and include:
       1. Quantity
       2. Price
    5. Confirm the receipt
    6. Click on "Place Order" to confirm the order
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Create bulk orders">
    1. Go to the Orders page and click on "Add Order"
    2. Choose "Add Bulk Order" from the dropdown, and download the template
    3. Fill the rows and include:
       1. Customer's full name
       2. Contact number
       3. Product SKU
       4. Address
       5. City
       6. Area
       7. Country
    4. Confirm the receipt
    5. Click on "Place Order" to confirm the order
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sync with online store">
    <Note>
      We currently only support Shopify and WooCommerce. We're working to support more integration platforms and methods.
    </Note>
    1. If you haven't already, connect your online store
    2. Go to the Orders page and click "Add Order"
    3. Click on "Import"
    4. Your new orders will show up automatically in the Orders page
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
