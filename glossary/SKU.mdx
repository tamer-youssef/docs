---
title: "Stock Keeping Unit (SKU)"
---

A unique code assigned to identify and track a each variant of a specific product in inventory. SKUs help distinguish items by details like size, color, or style, and make stock management easier.

## Examples

| T-Shirt | Small           | Medium          | Large           | X-Large          |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| Black   | FT-TSHIRT-BLK-S | FT-TSHIRT-BLK-M | FT-TSHIRT-BLK-L | FT-TSHIRT-BLK-XL |
| White   | FT-TSHIRT-WHT-S | FT-TSHIRT-WHT-M | FT-TSHIRT-WHT-L | FT-TSHIRT-WHT-XL |
| Red     | FT-TSHIRT-RED-S | FT-TSHIRT-RED-M | FT-TSHIRT-RED-L | FT-TSHIRT-RED-XL |
| Green   | FT-TSHIRT-GRN-S | FT-TSHIRT-GRN-M | FT-TSHIRT-GRN-L | FT-TSHIRT-GRN-XL |
