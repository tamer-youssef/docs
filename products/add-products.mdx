Adding your products to the portal is the first step to selling and shipping. This guide shows you how to set up product details, assign SKUs, and keep your catalog organized.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Add single products">
    1. Go to the SKU Listings page and click on "Add New SKU"
    2. Choose "Add SKUs Manually"
    3. Fill in the product details
       1. Your product's name
       2. Product images

          <Tip>
            We recommend you add at least 2 high quality images.
          </Tip>
       3. A unique SKU ID for each item

          <Note>
            An [**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**](/glossary/SKU) is a unique code you assign to each product variant in your inventory. It helps you track items, manage stock, and avoid mistakes during fulfillment.  Example: a green medium t-shirt would have the SKU ID`TSHIRT-GRN-M`, and the green large t-shirt would have the SKU ID `TSHIRT-GRN-L` .
          </Note>
    4. View your products in the SKU Listings page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Add products in bulk">
    1. Go to the SKU Listings page and click "Add New SKU"
    2. Download the spreadsheet template
    3. Fill in the product details in the spreadsheet
       1. Your product's name
       2. A unique SKU ID for each item

          <Note>
            An [**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**](/glossary/SKU) is a unique code you assign to each product variant in your inventory. It helps you track items, manage stock, and avoid mistakes during fulfillment.  Example: a green medium t-shirt would have the SKU ID`TSHIRT-GRN-M`, and the green large t-shirt would have the SKU ID `TSHIRT-GRN-L` .
          </Note>
    4. Upload the spreadsheet
    5. View your products in the SKU Listings page
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Import from online store">
    <Note>
      We currently only support Shopify and WooCommerce. We're working to support more integration platforms and methods.
    </Note>
    1. If you haven't already, connect your online store
    2. Go to the SKU Listings page and click "Add New SKU"
    3. Click on "Import"
    4. View your products in the SKU Listings page
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
