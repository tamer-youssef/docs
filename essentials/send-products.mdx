Sending your inventory correctly ensures your products are properly received, tracked, and ready for sale. Follow the steps below to prepare, pack, and ship your items correctly.

<Tip>
  We recommend you go through the [best practices](/essentials/reusable-snippets) before sending your inventory to ensure your shipment is processed by our warehouse operators efficiently.
</Tip>

## Create an inbound order

1. Log into your [Flextock portal](https://merchant.flextock.com/login).
2. Go to the Send Inventory page.
3. Click on the Send Inventory button (in the top right corner).
4. Choose "Add Manually" .
5. Add the quantity you're sending in for each item in the list, then click Save & Continue.
6. Choose "Request Vehicle" if you'd like us to pick up your items for you, or "Send Inventory" if you'd prefer to send your products yourself to our warehouse.
7. Review your order, then click on "Send Request" to confirm your shipment.
8. Track your inbound order in the Inventory page.

## Create an inbound order using the Excel template

1. Log into your [Flextock portal](https://merchant.flextock.com/login).
2. Go to the Send Inventory page.
3. Click on the Send Inventory button (in the top right corner).
4. Download our Excel template.
5. Fill in your product and order information in the template.
6. Upload the template on our portal.
7. Choose "Request Vehicle" if you'd like us to pick up your items for you, or "Send Inventory" if you'd prefer to send your products yourself to our warehouse.
8. Review your order, then click on "Send Request" to confirm your shipment.
9. Track your inbound order in the Inventory page.
