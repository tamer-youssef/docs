This quickstart guide shows you how to set up your products and inventory in minutes.

After completing this guide, you will have your products ready to start taking orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your products" titleSize="h3">
    Add your products with details and images to get your logistics ready, whether you're adding them one by one, in bulk, or importing directly from your online store.

    Learn how to [add your products](/essentials/code) from your SKU Listings page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your Inventory" titleSize="h3">
    Stocking up your items in our warehouse lets us deliver your order as soon as it is created.

    Follow our guide to [send your inventory](/essentials/code), the last step before you're ready to start taking orders
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first order" titleSize="h3">
    The last step to start business is for orders to get delivered to your customers.

    Take a look at how to create [create orders](sdf) to ensure you follow the best practices.
  </Step>
</Steps>
