## Stock Keeping Units (SKUs)

An SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) is a unique code you assign to each product variant in your inventory. It helps you track items, manage stock, and avoid mistakes during fulfillment.

## Best practices for SKUs

- **Keep them short and consistent**\
  Use 8–15 characters max. Example: `TSHIRT-BLK-M`.
- **Use a clear, consistent structure across all your products**
  - Product Type – Color – Size → `TSHIRT-BLU-L`
  - Category – Model – Variant → `SHOE-AIRMAX-WHT`
- **Avoid spaces or special characters**\
  Stick to letters, numbers, dashes, or underscores (✔ `TSHIRT-BLK-M`, ✘ `01-Shirt_BLACK@Med`).
- **Make them meaningful**\
  Choose SKUs you and can be understood at a glance.
- **Ensure uniqueness**\
  Each product must have its own SKU – no duplicates.

## Troubleshooting

- **Duplicate SKU**\
  This means the SKU already exists. Try a new one or adjust your naming pattern.
- **SKU missing in bulk upload**\
  Every row in your bulk upload file must have a unique SKU. Please recheck your Excel template.
- **Confusing SKUs**\
  If people can’t easily what product the SKU refers to, simplify your format and avoid random codes.
- **Product not showing in orders**\
  Verify that the product was saved with a valid SKU. Orders can’t be created without it.
